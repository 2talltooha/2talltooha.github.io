    Research interests

    I’m looking for an undergraduate research position in bioinformatics, molecular biology or health sciences. It would be my first, so I expect to start with the routine work and earn the rest.

    What I want to work on

    • Bioinformatics. I’m learning differential expression analysis by rerunning a published RNA-seq analysis with DESeq2 and comparing my results with the paper’s. I’d like to do that kind of work on a real question, next to people who can tell me where I’m wrong.
    • Wearable and contactless health sensing. Building Stillwave and the tremor device taught me that most of the work is finding out what a sensor really measures. I want to learn how that is done properly, with reference instruments and a study design.
